# After Effects Render Checklist

## Before rendering

- [ ] Confirm the correct composition is active.
- [ ] Check composition dimensions and frame rate.
- [ ] Preview timing, transitions, motion blur, and expressions.
- [ ] Check for missing footage, fonts, plugins, or linked files.
- [ ] Confirm transparency requirements.
- [ ] Check the work area or render range.

## Render and delivery

- [ ] Choose an output format suitable for the delivery requirement.
- [ ] Use an intermediate/master format when further editing or compositing is expected.
- [ ] Use H.264/MP4 when a compact review or social delivery is requested and the workflow supports it.
- [ ] Confirm audio settings if the composition includes audio.
- [ ] Render a short test when settings or effects are uncertain.
- [ ] Play the final file and inspect edges, alpha, color, and timing.

## Project handoff

- [ ] Collect dependencies when handing the project to another editor.
- [ ] Include a note listing third-party plugins and fonts.
- [ ] Do not redistribute plugins, fonts, or paid assets unless their licenses allow it.
