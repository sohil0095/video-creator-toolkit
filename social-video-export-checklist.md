# Social Video Export Checklist

Use this checklist before exporting a vertical social video. Platform requirements can change, so verify the destination platform's latest specifications before delivery.

## Before export

- [ ] Confirm the requested aspect ratio (commonly 9:16 for vertical reels).
- [ ] Confirm frame rate matches the approved timeline and source workflow.
- [ ] Check captions, spelling, safe areas, and logo placement.
- [ ] Watch the full timeline for black frames, accidental gaps, and unwanted transitions.
- [ ] Listen to dialogue, music, and sound effects on headphones.
- [ ] Check audio peaks and ensure speech is intelligible.
- [ ] Confirm the correct version and client-approved changes.

## Common delivery starting point

For a typical web/social H.264 delivery, editors often start with:
- Format: H.264
- Container: MP4
- Frame size: match the approved sequence, often 1080 × 1920 for vertical delivery
- Frame rate: match the approved sequence
- Color: confirm the project and platform color workflow
- Bitrate: choose based on motion complexity, platform guidance, and file-size needs

There is no single bitrate that is ideal for every video. Fast motion, grain, detail, and platform re-encoding can change the result.

## After export

- [ ] Play the exported file from beginning to end.
- [ ] Check the first and last frames.
- [ ] Verify audio sync.
- [ ] Confirm the file plays on a second device or player when possible.
- [ ] Use a clear filename, for example `Brand_Reel_Final_1080x1920.mp4`.
